# Projectile atlas generation

Generator: built-in ImageGen (`image_gen.imagegen`), with `transparent_background: true`. No reference images or extracted commercial artwork were used.

Original: `art-source/projectiles-20261017/projectiles-original.png`.
Runtime: `web/assets/projectiles/projectiles-painted-v1.webp`.
Preparation: `node scripts/prepare-projectiles.cjs`. The original stays intact; runtime preparation only slices, scales and registers its alpha artwork. Manifest: `web/assets/projectiles/manifest.json`.

## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production-ready transparent projectile sprite atlas for an isometric historical civilization strategy game, hand-painted realistic miniatures with textured metal, matching detailed painted buildings and ships. Generate ONE tightly organized sheet, exactly FOUR EQUAL COLUMNS and TWO EQUAL ROWS, eight isolated cells. The sheet should be landscape 2:1 aspect ratio, ideally 2048 x 1024. Every object entirely inside its own equal cell with generous transparent padding, no grid lines, no text, no labels, no backdrop, no watermark, genuine alpha transparency. All elongated projectiles point horizontally RIGHT, viewed from slightly above, shaded from upper left. Readable clean silhouettes, rich hand-painted material, NOT vector icons, NOT outline drawings, NOT flat SVG style. Cell order left to right top to bottom: (1) steel naval torpedo with rounded dark warhead nose at right, subtle brass seams and small rear fins/propeller, plus thin translucent turquoise-white turbulent bubble wake extending far left; (2) same identical torpedo shape, same size and body position, second distinct bubble/water wake animation phase; (3) same identical torpedo shape, same size and body position, third distinct wake animation phase; (4) a single solid dark iron cannonball, shaded round sphere, worn and pitted iron, no fire and no casing; (5) a single small pointed copper-jacket rifle bullet projectile, no cartridge casing; (6) a modern slender gray guided missile with fins, dark nose right, modest orange exhaust at left, NO huge smoke cloud; (7) an olive-gray finned aerial bomb with metal panel seams, rounded nose to right, tail fins at left, no fire; (8) a short medieval crossbow bolt with iron head at right, wooden shaft and modest feather fletching left. The first THREE torpedo cells must have matching body registration: nose near 85 percent of cell width, body centered near x=64 percent and y=50 percent, wake extending toward x=10 percent. All other objects centered in their cells and sized to occupy roughly 65 percent of cell width or height as appropriate. Do not include ships, launchers, terrain, water rectangles, explosions or scenery. Preserve transparent holes between fins and feather barbs.
