# Audio credits and licenses

Music includes traditional recordings and contemporary cultural-style compositions. These are not recordings from the leaders' historical eras. Regional playlists are artistic associations, not claims of historical authenticity. Shared orchestral tracks accompany combat.

All bundled recordings were converted to Ogg Vorbis, music normalized to -22 LUFS and sound effects shortened/resampled with peak limiting. No author endorses this game.
